# Chainloom product brief

## What the recent leaders teach
A representative benchmark of recent Android/mobile leaders shows two dominant success patterns:

1. **Instantly readable puzzle loops** — Block Blast, Royal Match, Candy Crush, merge games. They minimize tutorial friction and make a single move satisfying.
2. **Long-tail progression/live ops** — MONOPOLY GO!, Whiteout Survival, Last War, Gossip Harbor, Royal Match. They layer events, collections, social competition, and recurring goals on top of a simple core.

Current August 2026 U.S. Android grossing charts include MONOPOLY GO!, Roblox, Candy Crush, Kingshot, Royal Match, Gossip Harbor, Whiteout Survival, Township, Last War and Coin Master. Sensor Tower's July 2026 worldwide charts also show Royal Match, Whiteout Survival and Gossip Harbor among the top revenue titles, while Roblox and Block Blast remain major download leaders.

## Product opportunity
Instead of copying the monetization-heavy live-service stack, Chainloom focuses first on the part that must actually be good: the first minute of play.

Design goals:
- Understandable in seconds.
- One-thumb portrait play.
- Meaningful choices without a long rules explanation.
- Strong cascade feedback.
- Deterministic Daily mode for fair competition.
- Honest advertising: marketing can show the real game.
- No forced ads or pay-to-win in v1.
- Offline by default and no account required.

## Core differentiator
Most block puzzles clear completed lines. Chainloom adds **color topology + gravity**: the player is simultaneously shaping lines and building connected color groups. A clear collapses the grid, so a placement can cause a second or third clear without adding complexity to the controls.

Knots provide an escalating obstacle that prevents the self-cleaning board from becoming endless. Pulse and Shift create rare comeback decisions without selling power.

## Why this is a better *product hypothesis*, not a guaranteed hit
No design can honestly be declared “better than every top game” before real retention testing. The prototype is deliberately designed to test a sharper hypothesis: **can the instant accessibility of a block puzzle support deeper tactical replayability without ad pressure or a fake-ad mismatch?**

The next proof points are player data: tutorial completion, first-session length, D1/D7 retention, Daily participation, score distribution, and qualitative feedback from at least 20–50 real testers.

## Sources used for market benchmark
- AppBrain — U.S. Google Play top grossing / top free games, August 2026.
- Sensor Tower — worldwide mobile game revenue/download rankings, 2025–2026 monthly reports.
- Google Play listings/community feedback reviewed for representative friction around ads, grind and live-service pressure.
