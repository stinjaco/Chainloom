# Chainloom Privacy Policy — draft

**Effective date:** [insert launch date]

Chainloom is designed to work offline. Version 1 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device data.

## Data stored on the device
Chainloom stores game progress, settings, high scores, and Daily Loom scores locally on the player's device using Android local storage. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties by version 1 of the app.

## Accounts, advertising, analytics, and purchases
Version 1 does not include user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking.

## Network access
Version 1 does not request Android Internet permission and does not require a network connection for gameplay.

## Children's privacy
Because version 1 does not collect or transmit personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The final Google Play target-audience selections and content rating must accurately reflect the published version of the game.

## Changes
If a future version adds analytics, cloud saves, advertising, purchases, accounts, social features, or any other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.
