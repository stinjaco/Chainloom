# Draft Google Play store listing

## App name
Chainloom

## Short description
Place blocks, build color chains, trigger cascades, and survive the Knots.

## Full description
Chainloom is a fast one-thumb puzzle game where every placement can reshape the board.

Choose one of three pieces, rotate it, and place it on the 8×8 grid. Complete rows and columns or connect matching colors to clear tiles. Every clear drops the remaining tiles downward, creating opportunities for chain reactions and bigger scores.

As your run grows, gray Knots begin entering the weave. Plan around them, clear them in completed lines, or charge your Pulse for an emergency escape. Limited Shift tokens let you redraw a bad tray, but every rescue matters.

Play for a personal high score or take on Daily Loom, a shared deterministic challenge that gives everyone the same underlying sequence for the day.

Version 1 is intentionally simple:
- Offline play
- No forced ads
- No energy timers
- No loot boxes
- No pay-to-win upgrades
- No account required

Place. Connect. Cascade. Keep the loom alive.
